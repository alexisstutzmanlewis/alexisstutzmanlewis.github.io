My Science

I'm a genomicist working at the intersection of inheritance and neuroscience, with a background in molecular biology and computational analysis across mouse, human cell culture, C. elegans, and Drosophila systems. My Ph.D. work in the Dowen and McKay labs at UNC Chapel Hill showed that heterochromatic H3K9 methylation and HP1a are required to maintain global 3D genome organization. Now, as a postdoctoral research scientist in Bianca Jones Marlin's lab at Columbia's Zuckerman Institute, I study how generations remember the past — and how those memories become biology — by investigating the molecular mechanisms of intergenerational inheritance.
